# Code of conduct

Software Release Radar is intended to be a practical, welcoming open-source project. Participation in the project requires respectful and constructive behaviour.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss the code, documentation and ideas rather than attacking people.
- Give specific, actionable technical feedback.
- Be patient with people who have different levels of development or self-hosting experience.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's credentials, private infrastructure details or personal information.
- Keep issues, pull requests and discussions relevant to the project.
- Credit other people's work where appropriate.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following are not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats or intimidation;
- discriminatory or degrading comments;
- sexualised harassment or unwanted sexual attention;
- doxxing or deliberate publication of private information;
- deliberate publication of credentials or security-sensitive data;
- sustained disruption, spam or bad-faith participation; and
- personal attacks in place of technical discussion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, reject contributions or restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its community.

For sensitive conduct concerns, use a private GitHub contact path rather than posting personal details in a public issue.

Good-faith disagreement about technical decisions is welcome. Harassment is not.
